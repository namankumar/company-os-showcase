# Company context

This is fictional sample data for a B2B software company preparing a guided onboarding release.

## Product

The product helps operations teams collect and reconcile equipment-inspection data. A new onboarding flow lets a customer connect their first data source and see an initial reconciliation result.

## Operating objective

Make the first successful connection reliable enough for a small pilot without promising a release date before the workflow has been observed under real customer conditions.

## Current boundary

No external communications, customer changes, or release commitments are authorized by this workspace. It can surface a proposed next step and preserve its evidence.
