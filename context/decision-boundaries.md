# Decision boundaries

| Change | Who may approve it | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Change onboarding workflow | Product owner | A source-linked recommendation and implementation review |
| Expand the pilot | Product owner and delivery lead | Observed onboarding results and support-capacity review |
| Send customer communication | Human account owner | Reviewed final copy and recipient confirmation |

An AI may summarize evidence and prepare a proposed decision. It may not infer approval from a meeting note or its own recommendation.
