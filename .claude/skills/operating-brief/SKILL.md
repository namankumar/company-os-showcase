# Operating brief

Read `AGENTS.md`, then read `context/company.md`, `context/priorities.md`, `context/decision-boundaries.md`, relevant `sources/` files, and existing `docs/` outputs.

Refresh `docs/operating-brief.md` with: What changed, What needs a decision, Proposed follow-through, Known unknowns, and Sources.

Use `sources/path.md#Lx-Ly` references for factual claims. Keep observed facts, inferences, proposals, and accepted decisions separate. Only a direct human decision may be labelled accepted.

Do not make external calls, send messages, schedule work, alter external state, invent evidence, or promote a proposal to an accepted decision.
